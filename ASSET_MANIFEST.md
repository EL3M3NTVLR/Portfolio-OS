## 4. Bengali "Lyadh" AI Companion
- **Mascot Personality:** Laid-back, chilled-out ("lyadh") character reflecting creative flow.
- **Visual Representation:** High-detail vector SVG illustration with responsive animations for 8 distinct emotional states:
  - `idle`: lounging comfortably, relaxed breathing
  - `sleeping`: cozy sleeping cap/bubble, gentle zZz
  - `alert`: attentive posture with curious eyebrows
  - `excited`: celebratory bounce with starry eyes
  - `celebrating`: victory confetti trigger
  - `dragging`: floating airborne with dangling feet
  - `happy`: warm, beaming smile
  - `sad`: gentle pout with sympathetic tear
- **Audio:** Zero autoplay; optional pleasant click/pop cues on interactive gesture only with instant mute switch.
